## Full description

GMODE Trip Recorder turns a landscape-mounted Android phone into an offline-first trip and telemetry dashboard.

Record Street, Off road, Snow, or Water trips even when Home Assistant is unreachable. Every route point is saved on the phone first, with GPS accuracy, altitude, speed, bearing, satellites, barometric pressure, motion summaries, battery, and network state. When connectivity returns, authenticated Home Assistant synchronization resumes automatically.

The v2 cockpit features a dynamic 3D vehicle matched to the trip scene: Truck, SxS, Sand rail, Snowmobile, or Mini jet boat. Live pitch and roll, GPS/magnetic course, an attitude history line, and configurable orange/red stability warnings share one clear central instrument.

Choose and reorder 13 gauges, customize six side buttons to open installed apps or GMODE actions, select a colour theme, and calibrate pitch/roll zero for the phone mount.

Trips can be started manually or automatically after leaving a user-selected home GPS zone and optional Wi-Fi network. Automatic recording is opt-in and off by default. Export retained trips as GPX, KML, GeoJSON, or full-telemetry CSV.

GMODE has no advertising, analytics, account system, or GMODE cloud service. Data stays on the phone unless you send it to the Home Assistant server or file destination you configure.

Important: attitude and shock displays are situational aids, not certified safety instruments. Do not operate the app by hand while driving.
